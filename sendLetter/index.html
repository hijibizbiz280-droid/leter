❧❤️ For You ❤️❧
My Dearest,

There are words I have rehearsed a hundred times, yet whenever you are near, they dissolve into silence — because no sentence feels worthy of what you mean to me. So I write this instead, hoping that ink and paper can carry what my voice sometimes cannot.

You are the kind of person who makes ordinary moments feel like gifts. A shared glance. The sound of your laughter. The way you say my name. Each of these small things has quietly rearranged the furniture of my heart, and I find I would not have it any other way.

I do not know what the future holds, but I know this: wherever it leads, I want you to be there — not because I am afraid of being alone, but because everything is simply better when it is shared with you. You make me want to be more patient, more present, more tender with the world.

So this letter is not an ending, nor a grand declaration. It is just a small, honest note to say: I see you. I choose you. And I am so glad you are mine.

With all of my love,
Always yours ♡
